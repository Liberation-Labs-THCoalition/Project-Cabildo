# Cabildo User Manual

**For campaign staff who aren't tech people.** You don't need to code.
You need to know what buttons to push and what rules to follow.

---

## What Cabildo Does

Cabildo is your AI campaign assistant. It handles the boring, time-consuming
parts of running a local campaign so you can spend your time talking to voters.

- **Morning briefing**: What needs to happen today
- **Social media drafts**: Posts in YOUR voice, ready for your approval
- **Opponent watch**: Alerts when your opponent does something publicly
- **Walk lists**: Who to knock on, what to say
- **Fundraising**: Who to call, how much to ask for
- **News monitoring**: What's happening in the district that you should know about
- **Compliance**: Filing deadlines so you never miss one

## The One Rule That Matters

**Nothing goes to a voter without your approval.** Cabildo drafts content.
You review it. You approve it. Then it goes out. Never the other way around.

Every piece of AI-generated content includes a disclosure: "[AI-assisted draft]".
This is California law and it's the right thing to do. Don't remove it.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Feed it your voice

Cabildo writes better when it knows how YOU write. Collect:
- Your last 10-20 Facebook posts
- Any council statements or press releases
- Speeches or public comments

Save them in a text file (one post per paragraph) and load them:
```
Copy your past posts into a file called "my_posts.txt"
The AI will learn your writing style from these examples.
```

### Step 2: Connect your data

You'll need these (your campaign treasurer or party contact can help):
- **NationBuilder** login (you have this through the Dem sponsorship)
- **FEC API key** — free at api.data.gov, takes 2 minutes
- Your opponent's name and any public social media handles

### Step 3: Daily routine

**Every morning:**
1. Check the briefing — what's due today, what happened overnight
2. Review any drafted social media posts — approve, edit, or reject
3. Check opponent watch — anything that needs a response?
4. Grab today's walk list if you're canvassing

**Every week:**
1. Review fundraising call list — make 5-10 calls
2. Check filing deadlines — anything due soon?
3. Review your door-knock numbers — are you on track?

**Every time you get back from canvassing:**
1. Log your doors knocked and any notes
2. Flag any voters who want follow-up

## What the AI Will NOT Do

- Post anything without your say-so
- Pretend to be you in any communication
- Access private information about anyone
- Target voters based on race, religion, or other protected characteristics
- Generate content that discourages people from voting
- Hack anything or access non-public data

## Module Guide

### Morning Briefing
Your daily dashboard. Shows: days until election, doors knocked vs target,
upcoming deadlines, opponent activity, pending content to review.

### Social Media (Facebook)
Drafts posts based on your platform, voice, and current events. Every draft
includes an AI disclosure and needs your approval before posting. Use the
templates for common post types: door-knock updates, issue positions, events,
endorsements, GOTV.

### Opponent Watch
Monitors your opponent's public social media and news mentions. When they
post or get press coverage, Cabildo drafts a response focused on issues,
not personal attacks. You decide whether to use it.

### Canvassing
Generates walk lists from your voter file (NationBuilder/PDI) and
door-knock scripts tailored to the issues you're hearing about. Tracks
who you've contacted and what they said.

### Fundraising
Ranks potential donors by likelihood to give and suggests ask amounts
based on their giving history (from public FEC data). Generates a
prioritized call list.

### News & Sentiment
Scans local news for stories relevant to your campaign — your name,
your opponent's name, your key issues. Alerts you when something's
trending so you can get ahead of it.

### Compliance
Tracks California campaign finance filing deadlines and checks all
AI-generated content for compliance issues (missing AI disclosure,
impersonation, voter suppression language).

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Feed it more of your writing.** The more it has, the better it sounds
   like you. Council meeting comments, newsletter articles, even long
   Facebook comments — all good fuel.

2. **Edit the drafts, don't just approve them.** Add personal details,
   a neighbor's name, something specific from today. The AI gives you
   the structure; you add the soul.

3. **Log your canvassing notes.** The system gets smarter about what
   issues to emphasize when it knows what voters are actually saying.

4. **Check opponent watch daily.** Speed matters in rapid response.
   A same-day response to a bad opponent statement is worth 10x a
   response three days later.

5. **Don't over-post.** 3-4 quality posts per week beats daily noise.
   The AI will draft as much as you want, but your audience has limits.
